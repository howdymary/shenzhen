Companies & Places
Huaqiangbei Electronics Market ↗

World's largest electronics marketplace. Components, dev boards, and sensors across multi-story buildings.

HAX ↗

World's most active hardware and robotics accelerator. Sources, funds, and scales startups from Shenzhen to global markets.

Troublemaker ↗

Shenzhen-based makerspace and community hub for hardware builders and creators.

BYD ↗

Global leader in EVs and battery tech. See the Seal, Han, and Dolphin EVs, plus the Blade Battery platform.

DJI ↗

World's largest drone manufacturer. Flagship store at DJI Sky City with Mavic, Mini, and Inspire lines.

Dreame Technology ↗

Smart home robotics including the L20 Ultra robot vacuum and robotic lawn mowers.
